## 2. The 4 surveys (the unlock ladder)

Each survey unlocks the next reward right away. Every survey opens with this line: You get every reward for finishing the surveys, whether your feedback is positive, negative or mixed. Honest answers help us most.

### Survey 1: right after the quiz (unlocks the free results + Blueprint)

- Top line: About 2 minutes. Thinking about the quiz you just took:
- This system's capabilities meet my requirements. (1 Strongly disagree to 7 Strongly agree)
- This system is easy to use. (1 Strongly disagree to 7 Strongly agree)
- Was any question confusing or hard to answer? (optional: which one, and why)
- How long did it feel? Shorter than I expected / About right / Longer than I expected
- What did you use? Phone / Tablet / Computer
- Anything else we should fix? (optional)
- Why those two odd-sounding lines: they're a short, tested usability measure (UMUX-LITE, Lewis, Utesch and Maher, 2013) and only count if the wording stays exactly as published.

### Survey 2: after reading the results (unlocks the Family Map)

- How well do [Nickname]'s top 3 wirings describe them right now? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / Very well / Exactly
- For each of the top 3: Does [Wiring] sound like [Nickname]? Yes / Somewhat / No
- Is there a wiring we missed? (the 9 wirings, or None)
- How well do the growing edges fit [Nickname]? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / Very well / Exactly
- How useful were the curriculum matches? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / Very / Extremely
- Did anything in the results make you feel worried, or like [Nickname] was being labeled? No / Yes (tell us what, if you'd like)
- Anything surprising? (optional, a sentence or two)

### Survey 3: about 2 weeks later (unlocks advance chapter 1 + the Founding Families list)

- Which parts have you used so far? (tick all) Subject plan / Daily rhythm / Quick tips / Prayer page / Teaching together / Family week / Siblings page / Curriculum matches / None yet
- How useful has the Blueprint been so far? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / Very / Extremely
- How useful has the Family Map been so far? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / Very / Extremely
- What, if anything, have you changed in your homeschool because of it? (optional)
- What was missing or confusing? (optional)
- Would you recommend the quiz to a friend? Yes / Maybe / No
- Optional: a sentence or two we could quote / May we use your first name and state with it? Yes / No
- Optional: add my name to the Founding Families list? Yes, as: ____ / No thanks

### Survey 4: with the 30-day retake (unlocks advance chapter 2)

- Top line: This is a one-month stability check: we're seeing whether the quiz gives a similar picture twice. Some change is normal, because kids really do change.
- The quiz again, same questions.
- Has anything big changed for [Nickname] in the last month (a new interest, a move, an illness, a new baby)? No / Yes (what, if you'd like)
- Did you answer any questions differently on purpose this time? No / Yes (which, if you remember)

## 4. The testimonial disclosure

What shows next to any pilot family's quote, and the rules behind it.

### Testimonial disclosure

- Next to every pilot quote, same size, never behind a link: Pilot tester. Received the Learner Blueprint, the Family Learning Map and two advance chapters of Unschooled by Design free for answering our surveys, whatever their feedback.
- Every tester who finishes Survey 3 is asked for a quote, not only the happy ones, and nobody's reward depends on what they say.
- If testimonials ever show on a page, critical ones are not hidden.

### Where these choices come from

- Usability: UMUX-LITE, Lewis, Utesch and Maher (CHI 2013): two items, 7-point agreement, about .8 correlation with the 10-item System Usability Scale. Credit the source if results are published.
- Length: Galesic and Bosnjak (2009, Public Opinion Quarterly): the longer the stated length, the fewer people started and finished. So each survey says its time, and the most important questions come first.
- Testimonials: FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR 255.5 (free products are a material connection that must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously) and 255.2 (invite all, not only the happiest); FTC Consumer Review rule, 16 CFR 465.4 (no incentive tied to a positive or negative review), in effect since Oct 21, 2024.
- Retake: about 2 weeks is the usual retest window (Paiva and others, 2014), so a 30-day retake is called a one-month stability check and its results are reported as early, since some change is real growth.
