## 1. How the guide stays honest (and out of Google's spam bucket)

The rules every page follows. They come from Google's own guidance (read tonight) and the disclosure rules you already approved.

### Guardrails for every guide page

- We call these guides, not reviews. Google expects a review to show first-hand use. These pages pull together the publisher's facts and independent moms' reviews, so they say exactly that, and they never write as if you used something unless you tell me you did.
- Every page has to stand on its own: checked facts with links, pros and cons from independent reviews (never a publisher's own testimonials), who may want something else, and similar picks with how each one differs. That is Google's line between a helpful page and 'scaled content abuse', which it defines as many pages made mainly to rank and not to help people.
- My recommendation for search: a page goes into Google only when it has a checked price and grade range and pros and cons from at least 2 independent sources we could actually open. Thinner pages still show in the guide, but stay out of search until they're filled. Two sources shows it's more than one person's opinion and keeps most pages eligible at launch.
- No product photos until a publisher's affiliate or media terms allow them, and never AI images.
- Commissions sit next to the link (your approved 'Eden earns a commission'), sponsors get their own labeled box later, and Eden's Table is labeled 'Ours' everywhere it shows.
- Prices and dates: every page shows when it was checked. I suggest re-checking every price twice a year, in January and in July alongside the quiz's July retake.

Notes:
- Google Search Central, Spam policies, 'Scaled content abuse' (read Nov 19, 2026): developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Google Search Central, 'Write high quality reviews' (read Nov 19, 2026): first-hand evidence, benefits and drawbacks, comparable options, links to more than one seller: developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews
- FTC disclosure placement: the same source your approved affiliate note already follows (results page copy, section 6).
