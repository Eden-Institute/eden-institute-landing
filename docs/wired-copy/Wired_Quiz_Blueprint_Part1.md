## 1. Growing edges

This page shows the child's 3 lowest wirings, with the block below for each. Same rule as everywhere: we call them growing edges. The 'red flags and when to seek an evaluation' page of the Blueprint reuses the evaluation wording you approved this morning, so it isn't repeated here.

### Growing edges: what every mom reads first

Growing edges are the wirings [Nickname] reaches for least right now. They aren't flaws to fix. God made [Nickname] on purpose, and nobody is strong at everything. Interest can grow over time, in stages, so offer small, low-pressure tastes and watch what sparks. If one never does, that's okay too.
If a growing edge is really a struggle (reading that won't click for months, for example, even with steady teaching), see the 'When to look into an evaluation' page in this Blueprint.

### When Storyteller is a growing edge

Words may feel like work for [Nickname] right now. Reading might be slow to click, or they'd rather show you than tell you.
Small tastes to try:
- Read short, funny books aloud together, and let [Nickname] pick them.
- Let them tell a story with blocks or drawings before they write it down.
- Play word games in the car: rhymes, I spy, silly sentences.
- Keep reading aloud long after they can read alone. That's often how a love of words grows.

### When Organizer is a growing edge

Numbers, rules and order may not be what [Nickname] reaches for right now.
Small tastes to try:
- Cook together: measure, double a recipe, set the timer.
- Play board games with simple rules, and let them keep score.
- Sort the silverware or the laundry, or use a picture checklist for chores.
- Tie numbers to what they already love: counting animals, timing a race. A child can be capable at math and still not love it yet.

### When Designer is a growing edge

Picturing shapes, maps and space may take more effort for [Nickname] right now.
Small tastes to try:
- Play with pattern blocks or tangrams.
- Draw a map of your house or the backyard together.
- Build from a simple Lego plan, or arrange a dollhouse or a room on paper.
- While you read aloud, ask, "What do you see in your head?"

### When Maker is a growing edge

[Nickname] may rather watch, think or talk than work with their hands right now.
Small tastes to try:
- Do one hands-on thing a week: baking, a simple craft, planting seeds.
- Get outside to play every day.
- Give them a small toolbox of real, child-safe tools.
- Keep it unhurried. Hands get steadier with practice.

### When Psalmist is a growing edge

Music may not be [Nickname]'s first love right now.
Small tastes to try:
- Sing one hymn together at breakfast.
- Play worship or classical music during quiet time.
- Clap out the rhythm of a poem, or let them try a simple instrument with no lessons and no pressure.
- Worship belongs to every child, musical or not.

### When Connector is a growing edge

[Nickname] may be happier on their own, or with one close friend, than in a group right now. That's not a problem to solve.
Small tastes to try:
- Plan one-on-one time with a cousin or a friend.
- Serve together: take a meal to a neighbor, write a card as a family.
- Choose small co-op classes over big ones.
- Give them time to warm up, and don't push them to the front of the room.

### When Ponderer is a growing edge

Quiet reflection may not come easily to [Nickname] right now. They'd rather be doing, talking or moving.
Small tastes to try:
- Ask one bedtime question: "What was your favorite part of today?"
- Offer a simple journal with prompts, or one for drawing.
- Try a short quiet time with a picture Bible.
- Let them see you pray and think quietly. Children copy what they see.

### When Cultivator is a growing edge

Nature and animals may not grab [Nickname] right now.
Small tastes to try:
- Take a short walk with one thing to find: a red leaf, a feather, a bird.
- Put a windowsill plant in their care.
- Find a nature book about something they already love.
- Go at their pace. God's creation is for every child to enjoy, not just the ones who love bugs.

### When Seeker is a growing edge

Big "why" questions may not be on [Nickname]'s mind much right now. These questions often come in their own time.
Small tastes to try:
- Read a Bible story and ask one "I wonder..." question.
- Let them hear you pray about real things in your day.
- Talk about why your family does what it does.
- Faith isn't a wiring to score. It grows as you walk with them.

## 2. Stewardship and calling

Shown for the child's top 3 wirings. Framed as ways to serve now and doors to open, never as a career prediction.

### Stewardship and calling: the opening

Every gift is for serving. "As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God" (1 Peter 4:10). These aren't career predictions. They're ways a child wired like [Nickname] can serve God and others right now, and doors worth opening as [Nickname] grows.

### Storyteller

Ways to serve right now:
- Read to a younger sibling or a grandparent.
- Write a card for someone who is sick.
- Tell back the Sunday lesson at dinner.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: writing, teaching, ministry and preaching, translation, law, telling stories to children.
- "A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver" (Proverbs 25:11)

### Organizer

Ways to serve right now:
- Run the family chore chart.
- Help plan the grocery list and the budget.
- Keep score, and keep it fair.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: engineering, accounting, science, computer programming, medicine, running a home or a business with care.
- "Let all things be done decently and in order" (1 Corinthians 14:40)

### Designer

Ways to serve right now:
- Decorate for a family birthday.
- Draw the map for a family trip.
- Help plan the garden beds.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: architecture, art and illustration, design, the building trades, engineering, photography.
- "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house" (Hebrews 11:7)

### Maker

Ways to serve right now:
- Help fix something around the house.
- Bake for a neighbor.
- Set up chairs or tables at church.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: the skilled trades, farming, nursing and medicine, coaching and sports, crafts, engineering.
- "And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men" (Colossians 3:23)

### Psalmist

Ways to serve right now:
- Sing at church or for a grandparent.
- Make a playlist for a friend who is sick.
- Learn a hymn to sing as a family.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: worship ministry, teaching music, performing, songwriting, sound and recording.
- "O sing unto the LORD a new song: sing unto the LORD, all the earth" (Psalm 96:1)

### Connector

Ways to serve right now:
- Welcome new kids at co-op or church.
- Help a younger child who is stuck.
- Write notes to people who are lonely.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: teaching, counseling, nursing, pastoral care, hospitality, missions, leading teams.
- "And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works" (Hebrews 10:24)

### Ponderer

Ways to serve right now:
- Pray for one person every day.
- Keep a gratitude journal.
- Be the steady friend who listens.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: writing, research, counseling, prayer ministry, any work that needs careful thought.
- "Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O LORD" (Psalm 19:14)

### Cultivator

Ways to serve right now:
- Care for a family pet or a garden bed.
- Feed the birds in winter.
- Pick up litter on a walk.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: farming, veterinary care, conservation, botany, landscaping, teaching others about nature.
- "And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it" (Genesis 2:15)

### Seeker

Ways to serve right now:
- Ask good questions in Bible study.
- Pray with a friend.
- Help a sibling think through a hard question.
- Doors worth opening as they grow: ministry, teaching, missions, apologetics, counseling, any calling that keeps asking why.
- "And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart" (Jeremiah 29:13)

## 3. Praying for [Nickname]

The page opens with Psalm 139, then the prayers for the child's top 3 wirings, then one closing prayer for the growing places. All Bible quotes are KJV, like your approved write-ups, and every one was checked word for word.

### Praying for [Nickname]: the opening

Pray these over [Nickname], word for word or in your own words. They start with the truth of Psalm 139: "I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works" (Psalm 139:14).

### For a Storyteller

Lord, thank You for the words You've put in [Nickname]. Give [Nickname] a love for Your Word, and use their stories to point others to You. Make their words kind and true.
"Let your speech be alway with grace, seasoned with salt" (Colossians 4:6)

### For a Organizer

Father, thank You for the order and wisdom You've given [Nickname]. Help them see Your faithful design in every pattern and number. Give them patience when things don't go to plan, and use their careful mind to serve others.
"Through wisdom is an house builded; and by understanding it is established" (Proverbs 24:3)

### For a Designer

Lord, You made all things beautiful, and [Nickname] reflects You. Open their eyes to the beauty You've made, and let what they make bring You glory. Give them patience to plan well and joy in creating.
"The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork" (Psalm 19:1)

### For a Maker

Father, thank You for [Nickname]'s busy, capable hands. Fill them with wisdom and skill, like Bezalel. Teach them to work for You and not for praise, and keep them safe as they build, fix and explore.
"And I have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship" (Exodus 31:3)

### For a Psalmist

Lord, thank You for the song You've put in [Nickname]'s heart. Let their music be worship, and let it bring peace to others, the way David's harp refreshed Saul. Keep their heart tuned to You.
"teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord" (Colossians 3:16)

### For a Connector

Father, thank You for [Nickname]'s heart for people. Make them an encourager, like Barnabas. Give them wisdom in friendships and courage to stand for what is right, even when it's hard.
"Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another" (1 Thessalonians 5:11)

### For a Ponderer

Lord, thank You for [Nickname]'s quiet, thoughtful heart. Like Mary, let them treasure what You do and ponder it. Meet them in the stillness, and give them courage to share what they've learned.
"Be still, and know that I am God" (Psalm 46:10)

### For a Cultivator

Father, thank You for [Nickname]'s love for Your creation. Let them tend what You've made, like Adam in the garden, and see Your hand in every living thing. Make them gentle with every creature.
"But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee; and the fowls of the air, and they shall tell thee" (Job 12:7)

### For a Seeker

Lord, thank You for [Nickname]'s big questions. Like young Samuel, let them hear Your voice and answer, "Speak; for thy servant heareth." Give them a faith that seeks You with their whole heart and finds You.
"Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find" (Matthew 7:7)

### The closing prayer (every Blueprint)

And Lord, for the places [Nickname] is still growing, give me patience and wisdom to steward them well.
"Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ" (Philippians 1:6)
