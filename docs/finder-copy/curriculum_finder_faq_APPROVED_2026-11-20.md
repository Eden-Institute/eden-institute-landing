Q1. How do I choose a homeschool curriculum?
Start with your child, not the catalog. Before you compare a single curriculum, think about how your child learns best, what lights them up, and where they struggle. A curriculum that fits your child gets used; one that doesn't sits on the shelf. Once you know that, narrow it down by grade, subjects, prep time and budget with the filters here, read what other moms say, and try a free sample before you buy.

Q2. What does "open-and-go" mean?
Open-and-go curriculum is ready to teach the moment you open it. The lesson, the questions and the activities are on the page, so you don't spend an evening planning. Some curricula ask for light, moderate or heavy prep instead. Filter by prep time to see only the ones that fit your days.

Q3. Can I try a homeschool curriculum before I buy it?
Yes. Most of the curricula here link a free sample, trial or free lesson, and some are completely free. Turn on "Has a free sample or trial" to see them.

Q4. Can I use ESA funds for these curricula?
Often, yes. Choose your state in the ESA filter and the finder shows the curricula marked ESA-eligible there. Our ESA state guides explain how each program works and how to apply. Rules change, so check your state's program before you buy.
("ESA state guides" links /esa.)

Q5. Are these Christian curricula?
Each one was hand-picked: Christian, or secular that doesn't work against your faith. Turn on "Christian curricula only" to see just the Christian ones.

Q6. Where do the "what moms say" notes come from?
From independent reviews by homeschool families. Each card links its sources, so you can read the full review yourself.
