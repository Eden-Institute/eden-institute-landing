The science gifts that get used are the ones that come with something to do. A kit that turns into a project the first week, and then keeps giving them a reason to go outside, is worth more than a box that looks exciting under the tree.

Here are five I'd happily wrap, sorted by age. Every one of them works alongside a nature notebook, which is where the real learning sticks.

## For ages 5 and up

**A seed kit with a root viewer.** The <a href="https://www.homesciencetools.com/product/plant-root-viewer-kit/?aff=258&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=affiliate_hst&utm_content=science_gifts_for_nature_loving_kids" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener" data-cta="learn-science-gifts-hst-root-viewer">Plant Science Kit</a> from Home Science Tools comes with a plant and root viewer, dissecting trays, forceps, a magnifying glass, soil, and radish and lima bean seeds. Your kids open a seed, find the baby plant inside, then grow one where they can watch the roots. It's the perfect January project, when the yard is asleep. (Here's the [whole seed lesson](/learn/how-to-show-kids-how-a-seed-grows) if you want to see it first.)

**A nature backpack.** The <a href="https://www.homesciencetools.com/product/botanists-backpack-kit/?aff=258&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=affiliate_hst&utm_content=science_gifts_for_nature_loving_kids" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener" data-cta="learn-science-gifts-hst-backpack">Botanist's Backpack Kit</a> is made for ages 5 to 10. It has colored pencils, a notebook, a magnifying glass, a plant press, seeds, leaves, a tree ring, dissection trays, forceps, gloves and a scalpel. That last one is a grown-up job, so keep it in your hands. Everything else is ready for the first walk.

## For ages 6 and up

**Sun print paper.** The <a href="https://www.homesciencetools.com/product/small-sunprint-kit/?aff=258&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=affiliate_hst&utm_content=science_gifts_for_nature_loving_kids" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener" data-cta="learn-science-gifts-hst-sunprint">Small Sunprint Kit</a> has 12 sheets of 4 x 4 inch paper and an acrylic sheet. Lay a leaf on the paper, set it in the sun for a few minutes, dip it in water, and the leaf's shape appears. It's a small gift with a big wow, and every print can be taped into the notebook.

## For ages 8 and up

**A real flower press.** The <a href="https://www.homesciencetools.com/product/flower-press-kit/?aff=258&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=affiliate_hst&utm_content=science_gifts_for_nature_loving_kids" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener" data-cta="learn-science-gifts-hst-flower-press">Flower Press Kit</a> has a wooden press plus a notebook, cardstock, glue, mounting labels, envelopes, a magnifying glass, and a presentation book for the finished collection. Older kids love building a collection they can add to every season, and labeling each one with its name, the date and where they found it is real botany.

## A use-together tool for spring

**A bug jar with a lens in the lid.** The <a href="https://www.homesciencetools.com/product/deluxe-bug-magnifier/?aff=258&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=affiliate_hst&utm_content=science_gifts_for_nature_loving_kids" target="_blank" rel="sponsored noopener" data-cta="learn-science-gifts-hst-bug-magnifier">Deluxe Bug Magnifier</a> lets a beetle get a close look and then go back outside. Home Science Tools labels it ages 12 and up, so make it a together tool. Tuck it away for the first warm day.

## Make the gift stick

Pick the first project before you wrap it. Plan a day that first week to open it together and do one thing, then keep it with their nature notebook instead of in the toy bin. A gift that's used every week for a year is a better gift than one that's played with once.

That's the whole idea behind [Eden's Table](/homeschool), too: one real plant a week, held, smelled, drawn and named. These tools just give your kids more ways to look closely.

"Great are the works of the LORD; They are studied by all who delight in them" (Psalm 111:2, NASB1995). A kid who loves being outside is already delighting in His works. A good gift just helps them study them.
