Most families buy homeschool curriculum in the spring and summer, and the busiest weeks are late July through mid-August. But homeschooling lets you set your own calendar, so the right time to buy is the time that fits your child, not the calendar.

## When do most families shop for curriculum?

Searches on Google follow the school calendar. Averaged over the last five years, interest in "homeschool curriculum" builds from February through May, dips a little in June, and climbs to its highest point from late July through mid-August. It falls off in September and is at its lowest in November and December.

If you shop in the spring, you have time to try things before the summer rush. If you shop in late July, you're shopping with everyone else, so start your trials a few weeks earlier.

## When do I buy for my girls?

I usually start looking in June and have our choices made by the end of July. I include my girls in the process, and I consider their skill sets and their interests. If there's a way to try something first, like a trial class or a couple of sample lessons, we try it.

Once we've purchased a curriculum, we commit to it. But if it's really not working, or it's dreadful, we switch.

## Do you have to follow the school calendar?

No. One of the gifts of homeschooling is that you decide for yourself when your school year begins and ends, and how long each curriculum lasts. Point it to your child, not to the calendar or to any particular curriculum.

Check your state's rules before you plan your year. Every state sets its own homeschool law, and HSLDA suggests keeping attendance records in the states that require them. Find your state on [HSLDA's legal map](https://hslda.org/legal), and see [What do I need to homeschool?](/learn/what-do-i-need-to-homeschool) for the rest of the paperwork.

## How do you try a curriculum before you buy it?

Look for a trial class or a couple of sample lessons, and teach them with your child the way you would on a normal week. That tells you more than a catalog or a review. ## What if it isn't working?

Give it a fair try once you've bought it. But if it's really not working, or it's dreadful, switch. You can change in the middle of a year, and the break around Christmas is a natural place to do it.
